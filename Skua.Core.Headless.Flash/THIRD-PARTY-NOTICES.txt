Skua.Core.Headless.Flash - third-party notices
==============================================

HeadlessFlashUtil answers skua.swf's ExternalInterface callbacks from this repository's Skua.AS3 sources. Its call
table follows Seraph's app/lib/skua/swfworld.js (SwfWorld.answer / callFunction: which callbacks and game functions
a headless stand-in must answer, getTargetMonster/attack/useSkill/canUseSkill semantics, toggleItemEquip, the
RemoteRegistry link handling, Game.getDefinition("InvCat")) and engine/host/IpcFlashUtil.cs (how unmodified
Skua.Core is hosted on an IFlashUtil without Flash). Seraph was used as a read-only reference; nothing of it is
executed or redistributed as is.
